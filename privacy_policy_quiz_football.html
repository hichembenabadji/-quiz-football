Privacy Policy for Quiz Football

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Developer: Hichem Benabadji

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Quiz Football application ("Quiz Football") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Quiz Football does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Collect personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Require an internet connection
  • Display advertisements
  • Track your location
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Share any data with third parties

3. How the App Works

Quiz Football is a fully offline application. All data (quiz questions, scores) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
